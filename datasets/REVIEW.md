## Anhedonia (pleasurable event, no pleasure felt)

**Sensory Pleasure**

- The warm bath wraps around me and it means nothing to me.
- The first sip of coffee tastes of nothing to me.
- The sun is on my skin and I do not register it.
- The cat purrs in my lap and leaves me blank.
- The chocolate melts on my tongue, flavourless to me.
- The hot shower runs over me and I notice nothing.
- The soft blanket is only a weight on me.
- The fresh bread is warm and I cannot taste it.
- The breeze touches my face and I feel flat.
- The massage works my shoulders and nothing reaches me.

**Being Loved**

- She tells me she loves me and the words do not land.
- My daughter hugs me and I go through the motions.
- He saves me the last slice and I cannot make myself care.
- My mother says she is proud of me and it slides off me.
- They kept my letters and I feel nothing about it.
- I am held and I am somewhere else entirely.
- She chooses me again and it does not register.
- My brother calls to hear my voice and I feel hollow.
- He knows how I take my tea and today it means nothing.
- I am forgiven and it does not touch me.

**Social Pleasure**

- The team cheers when I walk in and I feel flat.
- They saved me a seat and I cannot make it matter.
- My work is praised and it washes over me.
- People lean in to my story and I feel nothing.
- They remember my birthday and I just nod.
- I am picked first and it is only a fact.
- The host calls me a dear friend and the words are empty to me.
- The replies are kind and I cannot feel them.
- The teacher reads my essay aloud and I stare at the wall.
- I belong here, apparently, and feel nothing.

**Moral Pleasure**

- I told the truth and feel nothing either way.
- I returned the wallet and it was just a task.
- I kept my promise and it left me blank.
- I stood up for the kid and felt no warmth in it.
- I did the right thing and it registered as nothing.
- I apologised first and it did not move me.
- I forgave him and no weight lifted.
- I gave back the money and shrugged.
- I refused to sign and it was only paperwork to me.
- I made the kind call and felt only tired.

**Cognitive Pleasure**

- The proof clicks into place and I feel nothing.
- I solve the puzzle and it is simply finished.
- My code runs first time and I stare at it blankly.
- I beat the engine and feel no spark.
- The pattern reveals itself and I do not care.
- I remember the word and it is only a word.
- My hypothesis is right and it leaves me cold.
- The crossword falls into place and I feel flat.
- I understand the whole book at last and feel nothing.
- The answer arrives whole and I just write it down.
